## Description

Paste this into the Description field:

```text
Build your deck. Choose your army. Watch thousands collide.

Chromatic 2 is a portrait-first roguelite deckbuilder about massive real-time pixel-art battles. Choose three colors, combine their armies and abilities, and fight through four worlds where every route, reward, and card choice shapes your run.

FEATURES

• Seven distinct factions with unique troops and bonuses
• 36 cards ranging from swarms and cavalry to mages, siege engines, and champions
• Huge real-time battles with hundreds — and sometimes thousands — of units
• Four worlds per run, each ending in a powerful boss
• Shops, treasure rooms, enchantments, upgrades, and card removal
• Runs are saved automatically at every fork in the road
• A touch-first portrait interface designed for phones

HOW TO PLAY

Tap cards to choose your front and back line, then start the battle. Press and hold a card to inspect it. Build around faction and class bonuses, improve your deck between battles, and keep your castle standing.

Chromatic 2 is currently available in English and can be played directly in your mobile browser. No installation is required.
```
